## Domain Consistency

Andon, Inventario, Salud and Notifications retain their existing ownership and lifecycle. Inicio reads each open condition from the owning API or the active Notifications projection. No state transition was added.

## Architecture / ADR Consistency

The implementation is web-only and reuses existing APIs, shell primitives and Team Mex tokens. No cross-schema join, endpoint, DTO, N+1 home aggregation or notify change was introduced.

## Scope Review

Flota/Logística, pricing, costs, maximum stock, ownership, priorities, SLAs, visit states and dual-stack notify were untouched. Pre-existing untracked shaping/spec/EWO files were preserved.

## Visual Evidence

Inline browser captures were reviewed at `390×844` and `1440×900`. Required PNG files under `docs/screenshots/` are not yet persisted; visual verification therefore remains incomplete under `docs/design/VISUAL_QA.md`.

The independent visual pass initially rejected mobile Inventario table overflow and the wizard footer being covered by the bottom navigation. Both findings were fixed and visually rechecked at `390×844`; the remaining non-green items are the missing PNG artifacts and the AC-01 contract blocker.
